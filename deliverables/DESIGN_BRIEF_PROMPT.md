## 3. ADJACENCY MATRIX (hard requirements ★, preferred ☆)

- ★ OT ⇄ Recovery: **direct door through the shared wall** — post-op patients must never cross a public corridor
- ★ OT ⇄ Scrub/Autoclave: gowning before entry
- ★ Pharmacy ⇄ Main door: on the natural exit path, counter visible when leaving
- ★ Reception ⇄ Main door: desk immediately visible on entry
- ★ Reception ⇄ Waiting: unobstructed sightline
- ★ Waiting ⇄ Glass facade: window frontage, morning/evening light
- ★ Nursing station ⇄ ward corridor junction: sightline to every bedroom door
- ☆ X-ray/Dressing ⇄ consult cluster (outpatient wounds) and ⇄ OT (pre-op)
- ☆ Common WC within 25 ft of waiting; ward WCs attached or paired between rooms
- ☆ Recreation/quiet seating at the far end of the waiting band, away from OT/consult doors
- ✗ Waiting/recreation must NOT adjoin the OT/sterile zone

## 4. CIRCULATION & ACCESSIBILITY

- All patient corridors **≥ 5'-0" clear** (main spine ≥ 8'; trolley route OT→Recovery→wards step-free)
- 5' turning circles at corridor junctions and inside accessible WCs
- Door leaves: patient rooms ≥ 3'-6" (D1), toilets ≥ 2'-6" (D3), OT ≥ 4'-0" double

## 5. SERVICES & UTILITIES

- **Plumbing:** gang wet areas on shared walls/stacks — ward toilet pairs back-to-back, waiting WC beside existing shaft, pantry on the north wet wall
- **Medical gas:** O₂/vacuum to OT, Recovery, and all 9 beds; manifold in store room
- **Electrical:** UPS ring for OT + Recovery; isolated supply in OT; bedhead panels in wards
- **HVAC:** OT on dedicated filtered AHU (positive pressure); toilets/dirty utility exhausted (negative); openable glass gives natural ventilation to public/inpatient zones
- **Finishes:** seamless vinyl + coved skirting in OT/Recovery/Dressing; antiskid ceramic in wet areas; calming palette (soft green surgical, blue consult, lavender wards, warm cream public)

## 6. WAYFINDING & SIGNAGE

Illuminated directory at reception; overhead direction signs at every corridor junction (pharmacy/exit, wards, OPD); room number + name plate at each door; floor-level guidance strips to X-ray and wards.

## 7. KNOWN FLAWS IN THE PREVIOUS PROPOSAL (must be corrected)

1. Recovery was across a 12' public passage from the OT → **relocate Recovery adjacent to the OT with a direct connecting door**
2. Pharmacy was buried mid-plan → **relocate to the exit path beside the main door**
3. Waiting area was internal with no window → **relocate to the openable-glass facade for outside views**
4. Restrooms/toilet distribution was acceptable → retain
