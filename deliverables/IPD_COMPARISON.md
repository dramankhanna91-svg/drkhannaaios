- **Envelope was already right.** Wall faces re-read from `floorplan/source/solaris_typical_5_7_10.dxf` (layer DR - PLASTER LINE): unit x05 is 13,210 mm = 43'-4" clear south to north (Y -221141 to -234351) and about 12,250 mm = 40'-2" clear east to west at its south wall (X 6002250 to the inner facade line extended, about 6014498), tapering to 10,512 mm = 34'-6" at the start of the rounded corner. Both models already used these faces; no room was drawn larger than the building.
- **Layout H** fitted five twin rooms (10 beds) only because the symbols were under-size. At true size two beds and two lockers need 9'-6" of head wall; the two northern rooms had about 11'-6" and 10'-10" between the toilet wall and the facade column, so nothing was left for a 2' wardrobe plus a 2' attendant sofa, and the foot aisle of the 10'-0" to 10'-4" deep rooms was 3'-0" to 3'-4".
- **Layout G** furniture was close to true size; the rooms read as cramped because each 9'-0" long toilet stood across the window and left an L-shaped room, and because the label (name, an "≈ average" size and area on a white box) sat on top of the bed. The printed "≈19'-2" x 10'-5" incl. toilet" was an average width, not a measurable clear size.
- **The architect's rooms are not larger.** His four west rooms are 9'-0" to 10'-1½" deep as printed; ours are 10'-3" deep or more. They look roomy because the twin-room toilets are in a separate 5' band, the rooms are plain rectangles and the label is small and clear of the furniture.

## 2. What changed

- **Layout H:** the west side is now three twin rooms 13'-7" to 15'-0" deep instead of four at 10'-0" to 11'-6"; with Twin 4 on the east side that is **8 beds, not 10** (architect, per the Layout H brief: 9 beds in 7 rooms; 6 of them in his four west rooms). Every twin has two true-size beds with 3'-0" to 3'-6" between them, two lockers, a 2' x 4' wardrobe and an attendant sofa. Three ensuites are 6'-0" x 7'-0" wheelchair size; ensuites of Twin 1 and 2 are back to back. Ward passage stays 6'-6".
- **Layout G:** the clients' four single rooms and the suite keep their positions and sizes. The paired toilets stay back to back at the glass but are 5'-0" deep and 6'-0" to 6'-9" long instead of 5'-5" x 9'-0", and sit on the bed-head side, so the foot side of each room runs clear to the window. The suite bath is enlarged to 7'-0" x 6'-0" (wheelchair size, 5'-0" turning circle); Store and Duty room are 7" shallower and the Pantry is 14" narrower and 7" deeper for it. Room 3's door moves to the south end of its passage wall.
- **Both sheets:** every IPD label gives the clear size (E-W x N-S) and the area net of the toilet, placed on clear floor; blue dimension strings run wall face to wall face in every ward room; toilets carry their clear size.

## 5. Twin potential in Layout G

Checked, not adopted. With the toilet at the glass, Room 1's bed bay (passage wall to toilet wall) is 11'-3" and Room 2's the same; Rooms 3 and 4 have 7'-10". Two beds with 3'-0" between them need 9'-6", so Rooms 1 and 2 would take two beds but then have no place for a wardrobe or an attendant sofa clear of the 3'-5" foot aisle, and Room 1 would give 77 sf per bed against the architect's 98. Rooms 3 and 4 cannot take two beds. A twin room in G needs the toilet moved to the passage end, as in H, which changes the clients' sketch.

## 7. Not verified

- The architect's room sizes are taken as printed; his drawing was not re-measured against the DWG.
- Wall faces are plaster faces from the builder's DWG, not a site survey.
- Drainage: only the east-wall toilet position of unit x05 is a builder's wet point. Every ward toilet in G and H needs a raised floor and new drain runs; this is not designed.
- 3'-0" between twin beds and the 6'-6" (H) / 6'-0" (G) ward passage are not checked against NABH or the local fire officer's requirements.
- The two feasibility briefs (`LAYOUT_G_BRIEF_AND_FEASIBILITY.md`, `LAYOUT_H_BRIEF_AND_FEASIBILITY.md`) carry a revision note at the top; their body text and tables still describe the ward before this fix.
- `flow_scores.md` and the 3D / blueprint views were not reviewed for the new ward beyond regenerating them.
