# GEOMETRY REVISION B — floor plate re-surveyed (22-08-2026)

Triggered by Dr. Khanna sending the builder's typical-floor layout (rendered
plan + DWG opened in a CAD viewer). Cross-checking the architect's sheet
against it exposed an error in the traced envelope.

## What was wrong

Revision A assumed the plate ran **101'-0"**. It does not. A calibrated pixel
survey of Ar. Bankim Dave's sheet — east wall x=226 px, south wall y=580 px,
north wall y=1779 px, SW corner x=858 px, giving 13.031 px/ft against the
stated 48'-6" south wall — measures the plate at **92'-0"**.

| Item | Revision A (wrong) | Revision B (measured) |
|---|---|---|
| South wall | 48'-6" | 48'-6" (confirmed) |
| East wall / overall length | 101'-0" | **92'-0"** |
| North wall | 29'-0" | ≈ 31'-0" |
| Width at 60'-0" north | 42'-6" | **39'-11"** |
| Width at 80'-0" north | 40'-4" | **37'-1"** |
| Gross area | — | ≈ 3,871 sq ft |
| Net area (less cut-out) | 4,164 sq ft | **≈ 3,729 sq ft** |
| Main entry, from south corner | 49'-6" – 53'-6" | **43'-0" – 48'-0"** |
| Cut-out position | 27'-6" E / 48'-0" S | **25'-7" E / 40'-0" S** |
| Cut-out size | 16'-2½" × 12'-6" | **≈ 18'-4" × 8'-0"** |
| Duct on east wall | ≈ 59'-2" | **≈ 51'-2"** |

Revision B was verified by drawing the polygon back over the architect's sheet
at the calibrated scale: the outline, the cut-out and the main door all land
exactly on the drawn features.

**The plate is roughly 10% smaller than Revision A assumed.**

## Service area — what the two drawings say

- **Architect's sheet:** a shallow hatched strip (≈2'-6" deep) along the SOUTH,
  outside the unit's south wall, labelled SERVICE AREA. It is not part of the
  usable tenancy. Separately, a large hatched zone on the west facade is
  labelled **CUT-OUT**, drawn with the diagonal-cross void symbol.
- **Builder's typical-floor plan:** the same cross-hatched zone in the same
  position, adjacent to the unit's corner toilet, is labelled **SERVICE AREA**
  — not "cut-out". Service areas on that plan are substantial zones, not
  ledges.

**Action for the architect / builder:** confirm whether that zone has a slab.
If it is a genuine open cut-out, nothing changes. If it is a service area with
a slab, roughly 145 sq ft may be usable (or at least usable for AC plant and
service runs), and who it belongs to needs settling.

## Consequences

- The **A4 outline sheets are corrected** (Revision B) and safe to sketch on.
- **Layouts A, B, C and D were all drawn on the over-long Revision A plate and
  are now invalid.** They need re-fitting to the corrected envelope — the ward
  wing in particular loses about 9 ft of length.
