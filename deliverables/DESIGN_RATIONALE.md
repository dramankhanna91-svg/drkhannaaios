# DESIGN RATIONALE
## Hospital of Dr. Aman Khanna — 7th Floor, Solaris Shine, Althan, Surat
### Revision of Ar. Bankim Dave's proposal dated 09-01-2026

Two layouts are provided on the same floor plate (~4,164 sq ft carpet, traced
from the architect's dimensioned drawing — the DWG should be used by the
architect to true-up exact facade geometry):

- **Layout A — Targeted Revision:** keeps every room and dimension of the
  architect's plan except the minimum set of moves needed to fix the three
  flaws.
- **Layout B — Fresh Alternative:** same plate and inpatient wing, but the
  clinical core is re-zoned for stronger adjacencies.

---

## 1. The three flaws and how each layout fixes them

### Flaw 1 — OT did not open into Recovery
In the original, Recovery (10'-5½" × 6'-1") sat on the west side of the
12'-1½" public passage while the OT sat on the east. Every post-op patient
would be wheeled across the busiest public corridor — an infection-control
and dignity problem.

**Fix (A & B):** Recovery becomes a 10'-6" × 12'-0" room immediately north
of the OT, sharing its wall, with a **direct 3' door** from the OT and a
second discharge door to the lobby corridor. It gains east-facade windows
(daylight for recovering patients) and grows from 64 sq ft to 126 sq ft —
now fitting 2 beds with monitoring. Trolley travel drops **20 ft → 2 ft**
and never touches public space.

### Flaw 2 — Pharmacy was buried mid-plan
Originally a 6'-9½" × 4'-1" (28 sq ft) kiosk deep in the plan beside the
cut-out, invisible on the way out.

**Fix (A & B):** Pharmacy moves to the old reception position at the
lobby/spine junction — every patient leaving OPD, wards, or recovery walks
past its counter, 8 ft from the main door. It grows to 7'-6" × 6'-6"
(49 sq ft) with a dispensing counter, rear medication shelving, and a
consultation window. Exit-path distance drops **36 ft → 26 ft**, and more
importantly the counter now faces the exit flow instead of hiding behind it.

### Flaw 3 — Waiting area had no outside views
Originally 17'-3½" × 12'-0" internal, boxed between the OT and the lobby with
zero facade contact — on the 7th floor, where the west glass is the whole
point of the address.

**Fix (A):** Waiting moves to the west openable-glass band (the strip vacated
by Recovery + X-ray + Pharmacy), ≈13' × 20' with 18 seats, TV, plants, and a
water dispenser, plus the existing accessible WC opening directly off it
(WC walk drops 34 ft → adjacent). The glass band is openable — natural
ventilation and full outside views.
**Fix (B):** Same position but extended to ≈13' × 27' (20+ seats) with a
quiet **recreation corner** (TV, plants, kids' nook) at the south end, away
from clinical doors — satisfying the recreational-area requirement of the
brief in the calmest corner of the public zone.

Reception in both layouts moves beside the main door (desk visible on entry,
8 ft from the threshold, sightline down the lobby to the waiting entrance).

## 2. What Layout B adds beyond the three fixes

| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| Scrub + Autoclave anteroom moved onto the OT's corridor wall | In the original (and A), scrubbing happens 30+ ft from the OT door across the south block; in B, staff gown/scrub and step straight into the OT. Old autoclave slot becomes Dirty Utility/CSSD. |
| X-ray/Dressing takes the old OPD-3 slot | It sits against all three consult rooms (wound dressing is mostly an OPD flow) and 20 ft from the OT — consult→X-ray travel drops 33/43 ft → 9 ft. |
| Nurses' Duty/Rest room (6' × 12') beside Recovery | The brief asks for a nurses' rest area; the station alone (A) has none. Bunk + lockers + desk, 15 ft from the accessible WC. |
| Third consult room = OPD 2 slot | The programme's 3 doctors' rooms remain: Doctor Rm 1 (attached WC), OPD 1, OPD 2. |

Trade-off: B relocates OPD 3, so if three *simultaneous* OPD clinics plus a
dressing room are all needed at once, A preserves that; B assumes dressing/
X-ray demand replaces the third parallel clinic. This is the main decision
point between the two.

## 3. Travel-distance comparison (corridor L1 metric)

Methodology follows LorenaPujante/HospitalEdgeWeigths (hospital knowledge-
graph edge weights = walking distances between spaces, corridor-mediated):

| Flow | Architect | Layout A | Layout B |
|---|---|---|---|
| OT → Recovery (post-op trolley) | 20 ft (public corridor) | **2 ft (direct door)** | **2 ft (direct door)** |
| Main door → Reception | 25 ft | 8 ft | 8 ft |
| Reception → Waiting | 24 ft | 46 ft* | 53 ft* |
| Pharmacy → Main door (exit) | 36 ft | 26 ft | 26 ft |
| Consult cluster → X-ray/Dressing | 33 ft | 43 ft | **9 ft** |
| Nursing station → farthest bed | 32 ft | 32 ft | 32 ft |
| Waiting → nearest WC | 34 ft | 2 ft | 2 ft |
| **Total key-flow travel** | **204 ft** | **158 ft** | **131 ft** |

\* Waiting is farther from the desk but has a clear diagonal sightline across
the open lobby/spine; patients are called visually. The trade buys window
frontage — the client's explicit priority.

- **Common toilets** — accessible WC at waiting + accessible WC at ward corridor + paired ward toilets ✔
- **Waiting 15–20 seats, water, TV** ✔ · **Recreation, quiet, plants, natural light** ✔ (south end of glass band, B)
- **Corridors ≥5'** — spine 12'-1½", ward corridors 5'-0"/7'-9½" ✔ (the architect's 3'-0" service passage behind the OPD row is retained as a staff-only spur; flag to the architect if patient use is intended)

- **Signage** — directory at reception, junction signs, door plates (markers on plan) ✔
- **Privacy** — all beds in rooms (not open bays), curtain tracks at twin beds; consult rooms open off passages, not the lobby ✔

## 5. Notes for the architect

1. Geometry is traced from the dimensioned JPG of the 19-12-25 typical floor
   plan; please true-up against the DWG (`SOLARIS_SHINE_TYPICAL_FLOOR_LAYOUT_PLAN`).
2. Structural grid, shear walls, and shaft positions must be verified before
   the Recovery/OT door is cut and before the waiting band absorbs the old
   recovery/x-ray partitions.
3. If radiography (not just dressing) is confirmed, the X-ray room needs
   AERB-compliant shielding; its position in B (inner, no window needed in A's
   slot) was chosen with that in mind.
4. DXF files (`layout_A_targeted_revision.dxf`, `layout_B_fresh_alternative.dxf`)
   are provided in feet units, layered (WALLS / DOORS / TEXT / FURNITURE /
   GLASS / PLATE / VOID) for direct AutoCAD overlay on the original plan.
