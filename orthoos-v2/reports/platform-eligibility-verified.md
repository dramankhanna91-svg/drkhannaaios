# Platform eligibility — verified for an India-based orthopaedic surgeon

**Researched 20 Aug 2026.** Correction pass after the first deep dive: the
question is not "does the platform pay physicians well" but "is it open to
an MBBS/MS orthopaedic surgeon practising in India" — and several are not.
Labels: VERIFIED (sourced) / INFERENCE.

## The verdict table

| Platform | Open to you? | What was verified |
|---|---|---|
| **Mercor** | **YES, via the international track** | A dedicated "Healthcare Expert — International Physicians" posting exists; MBBS or equivalent accepted; active licence + 5 years' experience required; pays India via Stripe. **BUT** the headline specialty rates ($130–400/h — cardiology, internal medicine, etc.) are for physicians **actively practising in the USA or Canada.** The international track's own rate isn't published — confirm it during onboarding before committing hours. |
| **Invisible Technologies (Meridial)** | **YES — explicitly** | India named in the eligible regions (US/CA/UK/EU/MX/BR/**IN**/JP/KR/SG); domain experts $35–125/h; apply at meridial.ai/projects; invitations reviewed within ~48 h. |
| **Alignerr (Labelbox)** | **YES — explicitly** | India in the published 14-country eligibility set. |
| **Toloka / Mindrift** | **YES — any country** | Join from any country; medicine is a listed domain; rates lower ($20–60/h) — volume filler only if it clears the ₹4,000/h floor. |
| **Outlier (Scale AI)** | **PARTIAL** | India is live (~50 supported countries) but task volume is prioritised to US/UK/Germany; clients can restrict projects by country; VPN use is an instant ban; ID + location verification at onboarding. Expect thin task flow. The radiology expert track marketing does not promise India availability. |
| **Micro1** | LIKELY | Healthcare/medical domain listed with live medical-expert postings; eligible-country set not published. Apply and see. |
| **Turing** | UNVERIFIED | No India-specific medical-role evidence found. Check current postings before spending time. |
| **Pareto / Sepal / Centific** | UNVERIFIED | Nothing conclusive found. Deprioritised. |
| **DataAnnotation** | **NO** | Reported eligibility: US, Canada, UK, Ireland, Australia, New Zealand only. **Removed from the plan.** |
| **Handshake AI** | **NO** | Program is for US-based candidates with US work/training authorisation. **Removed from the plan.** |

## The second gate: specialty demand (INFERENCE, consistent across sources)

Even on platforms open to you, most physician demand clusters in primary
care, internal medicine, radiology and cardiology. **Orthopaedic-surgery-
specific postings are rare.** You will usually enter under the general
"physician / healthcare expert" track — which means:

- Expect the **general-physician band, not a specialty premium**, unless an
  MSK-specific project appears.
- Your statistics training and editorship matter more than the surgical
  specialty for platform screening — lead with the full credential line.
- The specialty premium is captured in **direct consulting** (the 9 imaging-AI
  targets), where MSK expertise is the product — not on the platforms.

## What this does to the plan

1. **Week-1 application list shrinks and re-orders:** Mercor (international
   track) → Meridial → Alignerr → OpenTrain profile on Day 1; Outlier +
   Micro1 + Mindrift on Day 2; Turing/Pareto only if time allows.
   DataAnnotation and Handshake are skipped entirely.
2. **The ₹1.2–1.8L/month platform row in C8 carries more uncertainty than
   first stated.** With the US-track rates excluded, a realistic India-based
   blend is **$40–100/h on accepted projects** (₹3.5–8.7k/h). The row is
   achievable but sits nearer its bottom end until Mercor's international
   rate is confirmed in practice. The direct-consulting anchor matters
   correspondingly more.
3. **The ₹4,000/h kill floor does real work here:** Outlier generalist and
   Mindrift tasks can fall below it — log effective ₹/h from the first hour
   and drop anything under the floor at the 60-day review.
