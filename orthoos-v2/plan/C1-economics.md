## Market evidence (searched Aug 2026)

- VERIFIED: Mercor advertises $50–180/h for healthcare professionals;
  third-party reporting puts its top tier (~$175–200/h) at practising
  physicians and its accepted-contributor mode around $90–120/h.
- VERIFIED: India-eligible clinical/biomedical evaluator postings on the
  OpenTrain aggregator run roughly $25–100/h; generic India-eligible roles
  sit at $20–50/h.
- VERIFIED: Platforms pause or cancel projects without notice; some tier pay
  by region; several require time-tracking software (use a dedicated
  device/user profile); assessments are unpaid with low acceptance rates.

## Implication

- Minimum viable rate: ₹4,000/h (~$46). Below this, an OT hour is worth more
  than a remote hour. Decline.
- Realistic: ₹6,000–9,000/h (~$70–100) on expert platforms once accepted.
- Premium: ₹12,000+/h (~$140+) only via direct consulting contracts (rubric
  design, evaluation lead, clinical advisory), not rater work.

The constraint is hours, not rate. INFERENCE: expected remote income
month 1–2 ₹0–50k; month 3 ₹50k–1.5L; month 6 ₹1–2.5L. Sustained ₹3–5L
requires two or more concurrent sources, or at least one direct contract.

Rater work is a bridge and a credential. It is not the engine.

## The lever v1 left out: surgery

Formula: extra surgeries/month × your net surgeon fee per case.

INFERENCE (verify with your B0 answer to Q3): if net fees fall in the range
of ₹40–80k per arthroscopy and ₹60k–1.2L per arthroplasty, then 3 extra
arthroscopies + 1 extra arthroplasty ≈ ₹1.8–3.6L/month — the entire v1
remote target from four cases — and it compounds through reviews and
referrals. Every Engine A decision should be priced against this number.

## The bridge between them: the paid second opinion

Price to test: ₹1,500–2,500 for a structured written opinion + 15-minute
video call. RECOMMENDATION.

- Standalone: ₹1L/month needs 40–65 opinions/month. Hard from a standing start.
- As a funnel: if 50 opinions/month yield 5 patients who travel to Surat for
  surgery, the opinion fees are a rounding error next to the surgical
  revenue. The product's economics are funnel economics. Measure conversion
  from day one.
