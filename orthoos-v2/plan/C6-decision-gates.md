# C6. Decision gates and kill criteria

| Checkpoint | Pass | Fail action |
|---|---|---|
| Day 30 | ≥1 platform active or ≥3 in assessment; ≥5 paid opinions or ≥300 page visits; GBP calls above baseline | Fix distribution before building anything further |
| Day 60 | Platform mix chosen; ≥10 opinions delivered; ≥3 outreach replies | No platform acceptance → shift remote effort to direct consulting and statistics/methodology work |
| Day 90 | Remote run-rate ≥₹75k/month; ≥2 surgical patients traceable to the funnel | Remote below target → re-weight hours toward surgery and consulting; funnel below target → reprice/reposition before any product |

## Kill rules

- Any stream under ₹4,000/h of your time after 60 days.
- Any page set with zero leads after 1,000 visits.
- Any automation with no paying user behind it.
