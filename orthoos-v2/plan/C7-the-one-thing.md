# C7. The ONE thing

If I were Dr. Aman Khanna with these skills, constraints and infrastructure,
I would build the **paid orthopaedic second-opinion service as the front door
to the Surat surgical practice** — a concierge MVP on the stack that already
exists (one page, existing intake form, Razorpay link, Google Calendar,
written-opinion template, official WhatsApp). Not the AI front desk. Not the
dashboard. Not the full OrthoOS.

## Why this and not the others

- It is the only candidate that produces remote income no platform can
  switch off.
- It feeds the highest-value lever you have: surgical volume.
- It generates the de-identified reasoning material the AI portfolio needs,
  as a by-product of paid work.
- It is the first real use-case for the case-intake module, so automation
  happens behind paying users instead of speculatively.

In parallel — not instead — spend about 10 hours in week 1 on the platform
applications. The fuse is long and the cost is tiny. That is maintenance,
not a build.

Everything else in v1 earns its place only by passing a gate in C6.
