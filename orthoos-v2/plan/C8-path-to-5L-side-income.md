# C8. The path to ₹5L/month side income — where it actually comes from

**Written 20 Aug 2026**, from the B0 baseline and B2 scoring. Goal as stated
by Dr. Khanna: **₹5 lakh/month side income** (on top of the existing
practice base).

## The arithmetic that governs everything

You have **~90 hours/month**. ₹5L across 90 hours means a **blended
₹5,556/hour** of your time. That single number dictates the strategy:

- Rater-platform work at ₹4–8k/h *can* clear it, but hours are not
  guaranteed (VERIFIED: projects pause, pay is region-tiered).
- Second opinions at ~₹3k/h standalone *cannot* clear it — they only make
  sense as a funnel to surgery and as portfolio material.
- Direct consulting at ₹10–15k/h clears it easily — 35–50 h/month of
  consulting alone is ₹5L. This is why B2 ranked it #1.

**Conclusion: ₹5L/month is a 12–18 month outcome built from 3–4 stacked
sources, anchored by at least one direct consulting contract. No single
stream gets there alone inside your hour budget.**

(second opinions delivered personally, low-rate platform tasks) get
automated, delegated, or dropped — that is what the C6 kill rules are for.

## What ₹5L does NOT come from (so no hours leak there)

Per B2 scoring and C2's deferral list: resident teaching programs, pre-op
education products, faculty honoraria, medico-legal work (opportunistic
only), the AI front desk, dashboards, location pages, and any automation
without a paying user behind it. Any stream under ₹4,000/h of your time
after 60 days is killed on sight.
