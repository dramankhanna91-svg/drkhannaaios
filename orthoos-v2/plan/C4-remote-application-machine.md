# C4. Remote application machine (a process, not a product)

## Tracker (one sheet)

Company · Role · URL · India-eligible (Y/N/per-role) · Rate · ₹/h ·
Hours/week · Est. ₹/month · Ortho/clinical/AI relevance (H/M/L) ·
Synchronous time · Data restrictions · Conflict risk with employment ·
Status · Date applied · Next step · Notes.

(Template: [`../templates/application-tracker.md`](../templates/application-tracker.md))

## Cadence

Monday 30 min: scan new postings. Wednesday 60 min: apply/assessments.
Friday 30 min: follow-ups, log hours and ₹/h earned. Monthly: drop anything
under ₹4,000/h.

## Platforms to verify (eligibility is per posting, not per platform)

Mercor · Turing · Outlier (Scale AI) · Alignerr (Labelbox) · Micro1 ·
Invisible Technologies · Handshake AI · Sepal AI · Pareto · DataAnnotation ·
Toloka/Mindrift · Centific · OpenTrain (aggregator).

Existence and India eligibility verified in-session before applying; this
list is a starting point, not an endorsement.

## Direct-outreach categories (higher rate, slower cycle)

MSK/orthopaedic imaging-AI companies seeking clinical advisors ·
medical-education companies building orthopaedic question banks · CROs and
device firms needing protocol or statistics review · AI-lab healthcare
evaluation teams · journals and publishers paying for methodology review.

## The pitch (three lines, reuse everywhere)

> Practising orthopaedic surgeon (arthroscopy, arthroplasty) · journal
> editor · postgraduate-trained statistician. I evaluate clinical AI outputs
> for correctness, reasoning and evidence quality, and I design rubrics
> others can grade against.

## Portfolio (5 pieces maximum, synthetic data, one public page)

1. ACL injury reasoning with an explicit decision tree.
2. "MRI finding vs actual problem" — degenerative meniscus case.
3. Hallucination audit: an AI-generated orthopaedic answer annotated for
   errors, severity, corrected version, and evidence.
4. Documentation quality audit of a synthetic discharge summary.
5. A grading rubric for AI answers to "Do I need a knee replacement?"

Platforms run their own assessments and rarely read portfolios; the
portfolio exists for direct outreach and LinkedIn credibility.

## Rules

No identifiable data, ever. Check the employment contract before accepting
any contract. Use a dedicated device/user profile for platforms that install
time-tracking software. Never work platform tasks during employed hours.
