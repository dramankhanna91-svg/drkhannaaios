# C2. 90-day plan (one funnel, exact deliverables)

## Days 1–14

- B0 answered; B1 audit complete; employment-contract clauses read and logged.
- Applications submitted to 8–12 expert platforms (list in C4); all
  assessments attempted.
- LinkedIn rewritten: headline, about, featured section pointing to portfolio
  page.
- Portfolio: 3 pieces live on one page (C4).
- GBP overhaul done: categories, services, 10 Q&As, photos, first weekly
  post, review-request WhatsApp template approved.
- Second-opinion concierge MVP live: one landing page, existing intake form,
  Razorpay payment link, Google Calendar appointment page, written-opinion
  template, consent notice. Zero new code beyond n8n glue.
- Ortho content pillar added to the existing pipeline: 2 educational
  posts/week scheduled.

## Days 15–30

- First platform onboarding and tasks; hours and effective ₹/h logged weekly.
- 10 condition/intent pages published (v1's keyword list, but condition
  pages, not location pages). Each has: what it is, when surgery is and isn't
  needed, what the consultation involves, logistics for out-of-town patients,
  FAQs, author box, schema markup, WhatsApp CTA. No doorway content.
- Target: first 5–10 paid opinions. Record source and whether each converts
  to in-person.
- Referral loop: professional notice of the second-opinion service to the
  physician directory and referring physios/GPs (peer-to-peer, opt-in, no
  patient solicitation).
- Metrics sheet live (8 numbers, C5).

## Days 31–60

- Keep the top two platforms; drop the rest.
- Automate case intake v1 (de-identified pipeline, Module 3 rules) only if
  ≥10 opinions have been delivered — automate something with paying users.
- Follow-up flows on official WhatsApp templates: reminder, pre-consult
  instructions, post-consult summary, post-op milestone education, 6-week
  and 3-month recall. Opt-out in every message.
- Direct outreach: 20 targeted contacts (MSK imaging-AI companies,
  medical-education companies, CROs/device firms needing methodology review,
  AI-lab healthcare evaluation teams) with the portfolio link. Target ≥3
  replies.
- 8 faceless videos produced through the ElevenLabs/HeyGen/Higgsfield/Postiz
  stack.

## Days 61–90

- Scale what has data; kill what doesn't (C6).
- Product gate: choose between scaling the second-opinion service or adding
  a paid TKR/ACL preparation pathway bundled to surgery. Not both.
- Dashboard build only if leads exceed 50/month. Otherwise the sheet stays.
- Decide TallRise: plug into the same machinery or keep separate.
- 90-day review against gates.

## Explicitly NOT built in 90 days

AI front desk (v1 Module 2) · admin dashboard (Module 13) · location pages
(Module 8) · career "engine" as software (Module 10) · booking rebuild
(Module 5) unless B1 shows it is broken · more than one digital product
(Module 12).
